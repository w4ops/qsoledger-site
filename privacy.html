Privacy Policy

QSO Ledger keeps your log on your Mac. It never sends your contacts, callsign, name or location anywhere unless you choose to upload to an eLog service such as LoTW or QRZ.

If you turn on anonymous statistics, QSO Ledger sends only counts, such as the app version and total number of contacts, with a random ID. You can turn this off in Settings.
