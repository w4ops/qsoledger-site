Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

QSO Ledger keeps your log on your Mac. There are no QSO Ledger accounts or servers, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. The app collects nothing about you for its developer.

What leaves your Mac

  • Call lookups (optional): when lookups are on, the call you enter is sent to the service you pick, QRZ.com or HamQTH, using your own account with that service, to fetch the name, QTH and grid. Turn lookups off in Settings and no calls are sent.
  • iCloud sync: if you are signed in to iCloud, your log syncs through your own private iCloud account so it appears on your other devices. The developer cannot see it. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.
  • eLog uploads and downloads (optional): contacts are sent to LoTW, eQSL, QRZ Logbook or Club Log only when you set up that service and upload, and confirmations are downloaded from them.
  • DX clusters (optional): when you connect to a DX cluster, your callsign is sent as the cluster login, as every cluster requires.
  • Public data: the app downloads POTA spots and park names from pota.app and the country file from country-files.com. This happens automatically, and these requests carry no log data.

Passwords

Passwords for QRZ, HamQTH, LoTW, eQSL, Club Log and DX clusters, and any repository password, are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac, never in plain text and never sent to the developer.

Deleting your data

Delete contacts, ledgers or repositories in the app. Deleting the app alone may leave its data on your Mac, so delete your ledgers in the app first. To remove synced data, delete the app's data under System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud. Data you uploaded to an eLog service is managed on that service's site.
