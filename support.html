Support

Questions, bug reports or feature ideas? Email w4ops@icloud.com.

Community: ask other users and hear about new versions in the QSO Ledger Facebook group.

New to QSO Ledger? The Get Started page walks through setup, colors, fonts, and choosing your boxes and columns.

Moving from AC Log: export your log from AC Log as an ADIF file, then in QSO Ledger choose File → Import ADIF.

Backups: choose a backup folder in Settings → Backup. QSO Ledger saves ADIF backups there daily, weekly or on quit, and keeps the last 7 by default. Restore puts each contact back in its ledger.

Reporting a bug: email us your macOS version and what you were doing, with a screenshot if you can.

Call lookups are optional: name and QTH lookups need a QRZ.com XML subscription or a free HamQTH account. Turn them on or off in Settings. QSO Ledger logs normally with lookups off.

Privacy: see the Privacy Policy.
