Get started with QSO Ledger

Ten quick steps from first launch to your first logged contact. Everything here is also in Help → QSO Ledger Help.

  1. Enter your station. The Welcome window opens the first time. Enter your call, name, grid and location, and choose a backup folder. You can change any of it later in Settings → Station and Settings → Backup.

  2. Bring in your old log. In AC Log or any other logger, export an ADIF file. In QSO Ledger choose File → Import ADIF…. You'll see how many contacts were added, how many were already there and how many couldn't be read. Your awards are worked out right away.

  3. Connect your radio. Settings → Radio. Pick your radio and its USB port or network port, or connect through rigctld or TCI. Band, mode and frequency then follow the radio. For FT8, turn on Log contacts from WSJT-X and JTDX, and in WSJT-X set the UDP Server to 127.0.0.1, port 2237.

  4. Turn on spots. Settings → Cluster. Check one or more clusters, or POTA Spots for Parks on the Air. Clusters log you in with your call. Click any spot to tune your radio to it.

  5. Choose your colors and fonts. Settings → Colors & Fonts. Start from the Ledger (green and gold) or Navy theme, then change any color, the QSL row colors and the font sizes.

  6. Pick your boxes and columns. Settings → Fields & Columns. Choose which entry boxes show in the top and bottom rows, and which Recent Contacts columns show and in what order. Rec and Power are hidden at first; turn them on here. Drag a column's edge in Recent Contacts to resize it, and use Reset Columns to start over.

  7. Choose a window layout. Settings menu → Window Layout. Traditional puts DX Spots under the entry boxes; Side Panel puts spots and station details on the right. Uncheck Show DX Spots to hide spots, or drag the bar above them to resize.

  8. Log your first contact. Type the call and press Tab. Earlier contacts with that station show in Recent Contacts, and, if you've entered a QRZ.com XML subscription or HamQTH login in Settings → eLogs, your callbook fills in the name and QTH. Fill in the rest, then press Enter or click Log Contact. Press Esc to clear the form without logging.

  9. Keep separate logs. Settings → Ledgers adds a ledger for POTA, Field Day or a club station. Turn off Post to General Ledger for a ledger that shouldn't count toward your awards.

  10. Upload and confirm. Enter your logins in Settings → eLogs. Then use the eLogs menu to upload new contacts to LoTW (through TQSL), eQSL, QRZ Logbook, Club Log or World Radio League, and to download confirmations from all but World Radio League. For World Radio League, paste the API key from its Integrations → Developer API page, click Load Logbooks, and check the boxes to upload as you log and download every 15 minutes.
