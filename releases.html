Release notes

Version 1.0 (coming soon)

First release on the Mac App Store.

  • Entry form with recent contacts on top, country and zone lookups, and duplicate warnings
  • CAT control for Icom, Yaesu, Kenwood, Elecraft and FlexRadio radios, and Hamlib rigctld
  • DX clusters and POTA spots; click a spot to tune your radio
  • WAS, DXCC, counties, grids, WAC, WAZ, IOTA and POTA awards with maps
  • LoTW, eQSL, QRZ Logbook and Club Log uploads and confirmations
  • WSJT-X and JTDX logging, Net Manager and Cabrillo export
  • Repositories and ledgers, automatic ADIF backups, and ADIF import and export
