Release notes

Version 1.0 (coming soon)

First release on the Mac App Store.

  • Entry form with recent contacts on top, country and zone lookups, and duplicate warnings
  • CAT control for Icom, Yaesu, Kenwood, Elecraft and FlexRadio radios, Hamlib rigctld, flrig and TCI
  • DX clusters and POTA spots; click a spot to tune your radio
  • WAS, DXCC, counties, grids, WAC, WAZ, IOTA and POTA awards with maps
  • LoTW, eQSL, QRZ Logbook and Club Log uploads and confirmations
  • World Radio League upload and download with your API key; optionally upload each contact as you log it and download new contacts every 15 minutes
  • WSJT-X and JTDX logging, Net Manager and Cabrillo export
  • Repositories and ledgers, automatic ADIF backups, and ADIF import and export
  • CW: send with the radio's own keyer or a K1EL WinKeyer, with F1–F8 messages, a CW bar and speed control
  • Rotator control for Yaesu GS-232A/B, SPID Rot2Prog, Hy-Gain DCU-1, EasyComm and Prosistel controllers, or Hamlib rotctld; turn the beam to the call you enter or a spot you click
  • N3FJP-compatible connection, so JS8Call, fldigi and GridTracker can log to QSO Ledger
  • QSO Ledger API for scripts, Stream Deck buttons and other programs
  • DX Spots and the Rotator can open in windows of their own
  • Light, Dark or Match macOS, themes, editable colors and fonts, and your choice of entry boxes and columns
  • Free 30-day trial of every feature, then 15 free contacts a day for three months and 10 a day after that; a one-time $34 in-app unlock gives unlimited logging, with Restore Purchase
